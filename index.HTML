01 / Introduction

What is the gig economy?

The gig economy allows people to earn money through individual jobs, short-term work and digital platforms instead of relying only on permanent employment.

Our topic

The gig economy and Uber driving in South Africa.

This website investigates how Uber works, the opportunities it offers, and the resources needed to become a driver.

Focus question

How does Uber create work opportunities, and what digital skills, devices and requirements are needed to become an Uber driver?

02 / Abstract

What did the investigation discover?

This investigation examines Uber as an example of the gig economy. It explores how the platform connects passengers with drivers and considers the benefits, challenges and requirements of app-based driving. The research highlights the importance of smartphones, internet access, navigation and customer service. Although Uber may offer flexible working arrangements, earnings are not guaranteed and drivers must consider fuel, maintenance and other expenses. The investigation concludes that potential drivers should research local requirements, calculate costs and understand safety responsibilities before joining the platform.

Research summary

The investigation identified four key findings:

  1. Digital platforms connect customers and service providers.
  2. Flexible work does not guarantee regular earnings.
  3. Vehicle expenses affect the money a driver keeps.
  4. Digital skills, legal compliance and safety are essential.

My recommendation

Anyone considering Uber driving should compare expected earnings with fuel, maintenance, insurance and other costs. They should also confirm the current requirements for their area before registering.

04 / Career guide

What does an Uber driver do?

An Uber driver transports passengers using the Uber platform. The driver receives trip requests, follows directions, obeys traffic laws and provides professional service.

How to get started

Step 1 — Check requirements

Read the local driver, vehicle and legal requirements.

Step 2 — Prepare documents

Gather the identification and driving documents requested for your location.

Step 3 — Register

Follow the official Uber driver registration process.

Step 4 — Complete checks

Submit the required documents and complete applicable verification.

Step 5 — Prepare the vehicle

Confirm that the vehicle meets the platform's current requirements.

Step 6 — Start driving

Follow Uber's instructions and prioritise safe, responsible driving.

Requirements vary by location and can change. Registration does not guarantee approval or employment.

Smartphone

Runs the driver app and displays trip information.

Internet and GPS

Provide trip updates, maps and navigation.

Suitable vehicle

Must meet applicable platform and legal requirements.

Digital skills

  • Using smartphone applications
  • Reading maps and GPS directions
  • Communicating with passengers
  • Understanding digital payments
  • Protecting personal information

Safety responsibilities

  • Obey traffic laws
  • Keep the vehicle roadworthy
  • Protect account passwords
  • Avoid suspicious messages
  • Never handle a phone while driving

Conclusion

Uber demonstrates how technology can create opportunities in the gig economy. Driving may offer flexibility, but earnings vary and vehicle expenses can be high. A potential driver should understand the requirements, develop digital skills and plan carefully before starting.
